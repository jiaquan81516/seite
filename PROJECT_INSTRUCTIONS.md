# Seite project instructions

Target folder: `development/seite`.

Build a personal finance app. Use the supplied screenshot styling as the primary reference:

- White and warm cream surfaces with subtle beige outlines and soft shadows.
- Coral-to-orange gradient highlights, buttons and total cards.
- Large rounded corners, capsule switches and floating bottom navigation.
- Dark slate body text, muted grey supporting text, serif financial amounts.
- Pastel orange, blue, yellow and green category markers.
- Interactive charts and controls: day bars, donut slices, legends, calendar dates, period navigation, search, payment filters and merchant drilldowns must update the displayed data.
- Main sections: Entries, Bills, Plan and Summary. Include expenses and income, monthly budgets and savings targets.
- Include separate sign-in and sign-up pages. No authentication integration required at this stage.
- Keep forms usable on mobile and keyboard accessible. Label controls clearly.
- Preserve the distinction between a frontend demonstration and real authentication.
